bio:
  - "<b>Augusta Lardy Micheli</b> is a Swiss artist working between Paris (FR) and Neuchâtel (CH). She holds a bachelor’s in Philosophy from Kings College London and a bachelor’s in Fine Arts from City and Guilds of London Art School. Her work was featured in solo exhibitions in Madrid (Galería Belmonte), Paris (Galerie DS), Mexico City (Karen Huber), and Vienna (VINVIN). She is presenting her first institutional solo show at the Musée des Beaux Arts le Locle, Switzerland, this spring."
