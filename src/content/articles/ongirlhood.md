Girl 1 steals daisies growing inside her belly button and trades them on the train for sarcoline paper which, after some investigation, she finds might just be skin. *By jove, what a deal!* She sews her skin holes together and bumps into

Girl 2 who has always believed in nikhedonia. She likes what victory smells like. She will sink into the grass carpet in her car before she lets go of anticipatory success. Oh lordy, aren’t collarbones so sexy? She slides her hand into the space between her neck and bone. In the next train car,

Girl 3 is tired. She wants nothing to do with the other numbered girls. When they approach her, she innocently takes her toes off, balls them up, and throws them like little pellets. Once her toes are gone, she takes her nose off, packs it tightly, and launches it. But she too believes in nikhedonia, though victory has always eluded her. Her tonsil, kneecap, bone and finger (minus a pinky) bullets are effective and no girl will ever comfort her again. Meanwhile in the kitchen car,

Girl 4 cooks hair soup and hair cake with a hair ladle. Before she opens the hair oven, she thinly slips between kitchen tiles. She doesn’t return. Girl 8 has observed all the girls. Come, sit with me. This train moves at the speed of sleep. Because of this endless observation of the girls, her neck is a sliver, snapping from all the consistent circling. Around and around the skirt, her head turns. In the center of the skirt,

Girl 9 cannot stop drinking water. Soon, her stomach will burst in the ocean, and she will be absorbed, or it will be absorbed in her. In the milliseconds before, she ponders if she can reverse osmosis her way out of this mess. Just, then, a whirlpool opens up and twirls in the shape of

Girl 10, who notices the equations of the Fibonacci universe in the swirls of her snowy, inky coffee mixing and half of a plushy seeded strawberry.  She clasps all the girls’ hands (and Girl 3’s remaining pinky). She too exits the giant skirt. In the shape of a human, she stands atop the world-sized skirt and screams in the shape\
