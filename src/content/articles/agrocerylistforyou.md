(I wish I had a receipt to write it on. Perhaps then the romance of this act would be more romantic.)\
          ★   From the butcher counter—\
                    ○    Two ribeyes, one for me, and one for the cat in that window on Christopher St.\
                    ○    Chicken bones to boil for stock. There’s a long winter coming.\
          ★   From the pharmacy—\
                    ○     Advil. Living with you is intolerable.\
                    ○    Your anti-depressants\
          ★   From the florist—\
                    ○     Yellow roses. Oh, how you hate yellow roses!\
          ★   From the liquor store—\
                    ○     Bourbon and beer. I want to vomit. I want you to clean it up.\
          ★   From the bakery—\
                    ○     Day-old bread. I have an affinity for discarded things.

Do not take too long. I’ll be home soon. Your sister is coming for dinner. You know how she hates lateness.

I love you.

          ★   From the butcher counter—
