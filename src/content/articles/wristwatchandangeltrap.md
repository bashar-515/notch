**Wristwatch**
 
Tapped like the victim of an agent a sunset
skier’s dream range of mountain all peachy glory
 
heights of hip, knee, toe bone completely
wet and heard a seashell brought to an ear 
 
taken from the sweet sleep of your pocket I’m tapped
like a maple—slow, frequent, a prototype testing violent waters
 
weld it new blades or season the oars they breathe
each crashing dynamic with sea lungs every lost sailor practicing patience
 
waiting at the waterfloor or inside some gilled
beast all those men now memory and flavor
 
the drawbridge letting down on the bay casts shadows like imagined airships
when you look away and the instruments crafted are salted into protection
 
heavy my heart stands in place and waits I’m where you left
it my waist echoes in widening controlled ohs
 
like the lake’s surface calls outwards from a trout’s dive or a child’s
skipping stone. You say to spread like water and keep making guns
 
with your hands I keep
only seeing the handles so handless placated you’re a clock broken
 
some metal piece purely to touch and hold your heat becomes time 
a telling offspring a little devil rollicking
 
in June’s languid enormity I’m just in a gold wristwatch I’m just
unable to put more on with you I only have hours
 
you’re a careful hunter from a long line of them
in the glade you two-step creep slow kissing one eye to scope
 
the other closed so you see me with one and with
the other another thing you want focused or drawn
 
from past sometimes I feel so full of slaughter I’m maybe
part of your family I’m maybe part of mine rabbit or woven skin wearer
 
I’m so without warning a slab warm
from sun I’m so without warning in love
